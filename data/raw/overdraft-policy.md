# Overdraft Policy

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-POL-002 · Effective date: 1 March 2026 · Owner: Retail Lending

## Purpose

This policy explains when Fernhollow Bank pays transactions that exceed the available balance of a personal current account, what this costs, and how customers can avoid overdraft charges.

## Overdraft Options

Customers can choose one of three options for each current account:

1. **No overdraft (default for new accounts).** Transactions that would overdraw the account are declined. No overdraft fees are charged.
2. **Overdraft protection transfer.** Funds are automatically moved from a linked Fernhollow savings account to cover the shortfall. A transfer fee of $5.00 applies per day on which a transfer occurs.
3. **Arranged overdraft.** An approved credit limit of between $250 and $5,000, subject to an affordability assessment.

Customers can change their option at any time in online banking or the mobile app. Changes take effect from the next business day.

## Arranged Overdraft Charges

- Interest is charged on the overdrawn balance at an annual rate of 19.9%, calculated daily and debited monthly.
- There is no interest on the first $50 of an arranged overdraft (the "interest-free buffer").
- There are no fixed monthly fees for having an arranged overdraft facility.

## Unarranged Overdrafts

If a transaction takes the account beyond its arranged limit, or overdraws an account without an arranged overdraft, Fernhollow will normally decline it. Where a payment cannot be declined (for example, some offline card transactions), the account may become overdrawn without an arrangement. In that case:

- A fee of $25.00 is charged per item, up to a maximum of two fees per day.
- No fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10.00 or less at the end of the business day.
- No fee is charged if the customer brings the account back within its limit by 11:59 pm Eastern Time on the same business day.

## Notifications

Customers with the mobile app receive an alert when their balance falls below $100 and when the account becomes overdrawn. Alerts can be configured but the overdrawn alert cannot be turned off.

## Customers in Financial Difficulty

Customers who are struggling to repay an overdraft should contact the Financial Support Team. Options may include freezing interest and fees for up to 90 days, an agreed repayment plan, or converting the overdraft into a fixed-term personal loan at a lower rate. Fernhollow will not reduce or remove an arranged overdraft limit without giving at least 30 days' notice, except where required by law or in cases of suspected fraud.
