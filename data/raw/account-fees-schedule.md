# Personal Account Fees Schedule

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-POL-001 · Effective date: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Retail Products

## Overview

This schedule lists the standard fees for Fernhollow Bank personal current and savings accounts. Fees are shown in US dollars (USD). We will give customers at least 30 days' notice before introducing a new fee or increasing an existing one.

## Monthly Account Maintenance Fees

- **Everyday Current Account:** $5.00 per month. The fee is waived in any month where the account receives at least $1,000 in deposits, or where the customer maintains a minimum daily balance of $1,500.
- **Student Current Account:** No monthly fee for customers aged 18 to 25 who are enrolled in full-time education.
- **Premier Current Account:** $15.00 per month. The fee is waived where combined balances across all Fernhollow accounts exceed $25,000.
- **Savings accounts:** No monthly maintenance fee.

## ATM and Cash Withdrawal Fees

- Withdrawals at Fernhollow ATMs are free.
- Withdrawals at other domestic ATMs cost $2.50 per transaction. Premier customers receive up to five fee-free withdrawals at other banks' ATMs each month.
- International ATM withdrawals cost $3.00 per transaction plus the foreign transaction fee described below.
- The ATM operator may charge its own fee in addition to Fernhollow fees.

## Foreign Transaction Fees

Card purchases and ATM withdrawals made in a currency other than USD incur a foreign transaction fee of 2.75% of the transaction amount. Premier Current Account debit cards have no foreign transaction fee.

## Other Service Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Replacement debit card (standard delivery) | Free |
| Replacement debit card (express delivery) | $15.00 |
| Paper statement (per statement) | $2.00 |
| Stop payment request | $20.00 |
| Returned deposited item | $12.00 |
| Account research (per hour, minimum one hour) | $25.00 |

Electronic statements are always free.

## Fee Refunds

A customer who believes a fee was charged in error may request a review through online banking, the mobile app, or by calling customer service. Fees charged in error are refunded within 5 business days of confirmation. Fernhollow may, at its discretion, refund one overdraft or ATM fee per calendar year as a goodwill gesture.
