# Account Opening and Identity Verification (KYC)

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-POL-004 · Effective date: 1 February 2026 · Owner: Financial Crime Compliance

## Purpose

Fernhollow Bank is required to verify the identity of every customer before opening an account. This "Know Your Customer" (KYC) process helps prevent fraud, money laundering and the financing of illegal activity.

## Eligibility

To open a personal account, an applicant must:

- Be at least 18 years old (or 16 for the Student Current Account, with a parent or guardian as co-signer).
- Have a residential address in a country where Fernhollow operates.
- Provide a valid tax identification number.

## Accepted Identity Documents

Applicants must provide one primary photo identity document:

- Valid passport.
- Valid national identity card.
- Valid driving licence with photograph.

Expired documents are not accepted. Documents must be in the applicant's current legal name.

## Proof of Address

Applicants must also provide one proof of address dated within the last **3 months**, such as:

- A utility bill (electricity, gas, water or landline internet).
- A bank or credit card statement from another financial institution.
- A tax authority letter.
- A tenancy agreement signed within the last 12 months.

Mobile phone bills and screenshots of online documents are not accepted as proof of address.

## Online Verification

Applicants applying through the mobile app complete identity verification by photographing their identity document and recording a short selfie video. Most applications are verified automatically within 10 minutes. Where automatic verification fails, an analyst reviews the application within 2 business days and may request additional documents.

## Enhanced Due Diligence

Additional checks, including questions about the source of funds and expected account activity, are required for:

- Applicants who are politically exposed persons (PEPs) or their close associates.
- Applicants expecting to deposit more than $50,000 within the first 3 months.
- Applicants with connections to high-risk jurisdictions.

## Ongoing Reviews

Fernhollow periodically asks existing customers to confirm or update their information. Customers who do not respond within 60 days of a request may have their account restricted until the information is provided.

## Refusals

Fernhollow may decline to open an account if identity cannot be verified or if the application does not meet the bank's risk requirements. For legal reasons, Fernhollow is not always able to explain why an application was declined.
