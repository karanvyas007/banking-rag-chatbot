# Customer Complaints Handling Policy

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-POL-007 · Effective date: 1 June 2026 · Owner: Customer Experience

## Our Commitment

Fernhollow Bank aims to resolve complaints fairly, promptly and free of charge. Making a complaint will not affect how we treat a customer in future.

## How to Make a Complaint

Customers can complain:

- In the mobile app or online banking, under **Help > Make a complaint**.
- By phone to customer service.
- In any Fernhollow branch.
- In writing to Fernhollow Bank Customer Relations.

A complaint can also be made by an authorised representative, such as a family member or legal adviser, with the customer's consent.

## What Happens Next

1. **Acknowledgement:** we acknowledge every complaint within 2 business days and give the name of the person handling it.
2. **Investigation:** we review account records, call recordings and any evidence the customer provides.
3. **Final response:** we aim to send a final response within 15 business days. If we need longer, we will explain why and give a final response no later than 8 weeks after the complaint was received.

Many complaints are resolved within 3 business days. In that case we confirm the resolution in writing.

## Putting Things Right

If we have made a mistake, we will:

- Correct the error and refund any fees, charges or interest caused by it.
- Compensate for direct financial losses.
- Consider a payment for distress and inconvenience where appropriate.
- Apologise and explain what we are doing to prevent it from happening again.

## Escalation

If a customer is unhappy with our final response, or we have not responded within 8 weeks, they can refer the complaint to the independent Banking Ombudsman (a fictional body in this scenario) free of charge. Referrals must be made within 6 months of our final response.

## Record Keeping

Fernhollow keeps records of all complaints for at least 5 years and uses them to identify recurring problems and improve its products and services.
