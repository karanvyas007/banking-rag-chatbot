# Fraud and Account Security

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-POL-006 · Effective date: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Fraud Prevention

## Reporting Suspected Fraud

Customers who suspect fraud on their account, or who have lost a card or had it stolen, should contact Fernhollow immediately:

- Freeze the card instantly in the mobile app under **Cards > Freeze card**.
- Call the 24/7 Fraud Line on the number printed on the back of the card or on the Fernhollow website.

Freezing a card blocks new card payments and ATM withdrawals but does not stop direct debits or standing orders.

## What Fernhollow Will Never Ask

Fernhollow will never ask customers to:

- Share their full online banking password, PIN or one-time passcode.
- Move money to a "safe account".
- Install remote-access software on their device.
- Confirm their card details through a link in a text message or email.

Anyone making these requests is likely to be a fraudster, even if the caller ID shows a Fernhollow number.

## Customer Liability for Unauthorised Transactions

If a customer reports an unauthorised transaction promptly, Fernhollow will refund the amount, including any related fees and interest, normally by the end of the next business day. A customer may be liable for losses if they acted fraudulently or, with gross negligence, failed to keep their security details safe.

## Authorised Push Payment (APP) Scams

An APP scam is when a customer is tricked into sending money to a fraudster. Customers who believe they have been scammed should report it as soon as possible. Fernhollow will:

1. Attempt to recover funds from the receiving bank.
2. Investigate the case and respond within 15 business days (up to 35 business days in complex cases).
3. Reimburse eligible victims in line with Fernhollow's APP Scam Reimbursement Standard, up to $100,000 per claim.

Claims must be made within 13 months of the last payment in the scam.

## Security Features

- Two-step verification is required for new devices and for payments to new recipients.
- Customers receive real-time alerts for card payments over $250 and all international transfers.
- Fernhollow may temporarily block a payment it considers unusual and contact the customer to confirm it.

## Protecting Your Account

Customers should use a unique password for online banking, keep their devices updated, and enable biometric login in the mobile app. Customers should check statements regularly and report anything they do not recognise.
