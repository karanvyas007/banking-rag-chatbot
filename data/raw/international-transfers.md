# International Payments and Transfers

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-POL-005 · Effective date: 1 May 2026 · Owner: Payments Operations

## Overview

Fernhollow customers can send money to bank accounts in more than 60 countries from the mobile app, online banking or a branch. Payments can be sent in USD or converted into the recipient's local currency.

## Information Needed

To send an international transfer, customers need:

- The recipient's full name and address.
- The recipient's account number or IBAN.
- The recipient bank's SWIFT/BIC code.
- The purpose of the payment, for transfers above $10,000.

Incorrect details can cause delays, additional charges or a payment being returned.

## Fees

| Transfer type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Online or mobile, in recipient's local currency | $5.00 |
| Online or mobile, sent in USD | $15.00 |
| Branch-initiated transfer | $30.00 |
| Amendment or recall of a sent payment | $25.00 |

Intermediary or recipient banks may deduct their own charges. Customers can choose the "OUR" charging option, where the sender pays all charges, for an additional $20.00.

## Exchange Rates

When a payment is converted, Fernhollow applies its published exchange rate at the time the payment is processed. This rate includes a margin of up to 1.5% over the mid-market rate. The exact rate and the amount the recipient will receive are shown before the customer confirms the payment.

## Limits

- Daily limit for online and mobile international transfers: $25,000.
- Transfers above this limit must be arranged in a branch or through Premier relationship managers.
- First-time payments to a new recipient are limited to $5,000 for 24 hours as a fraud prevention measure.

## Processing Times

Payments submitted before 3:00 pm Eastern Time on a business day are normally sent the same day. Most transfers arrive within 1 to 3 business days, depending on the destination country and intermediary banks.

## Cancelling or Recalling a Payment

A transfer can be cancelled free of charge if it has not yet been processed. Once sent, Fernhollow can request a recall from the recipient bank for a fee of $25.00, but cannot guarantee the funds will be returned.

## Sanctions and Compliance Checks

All international payments are screened against sanctions lists. Payments may be delayed, rejected or reported to authorities where required by law. Fernhollow does not send payments to countries or individuals subject to sanctions.
