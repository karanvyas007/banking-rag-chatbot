# Savings Accounts FAQ

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-FAQ-001 · Last reviewed: 1 April 2026 · Owner: Retail Products

## What savings accounts does Fernhollow offer?

- **Easy Access Saver:** variable interest at 3.10% AER, unlimited withdrawals, no minimum balance.
- **Bonus Saver:** variable interest at 4.25% AER in any month with no withdrawals; 1.50% AER in months with one or more withdrawals.
- **12-Month Fixed Saver:** fixed interest at 4.60% AER for 12 months. Minimum deposit $1,000.
- **Young Saver:** for customers under 18, variable interest at 4.00% AER on balances up to $5,000.

Rates shown are illustrative and are reviewed regularly.

## How is interest calculated and paid?

Interest is calculated daily on the end-of-day balance. For variable-rate accounts, interest is paid monthly on the first business day of the month. For the 12-Month Fixed Saver, interest is paid at maturity, or monthly if the customer chooses this option when opening the account (the monthly option has a slightly lower rate).

## Can I withdraw money from a fixed-rate account early?

Early withdrawals from the 12-Month Fixed Saver are not normally permitted. In cases of hardship, such as bereavement or serious illness, Fernhollow may allow early closure with a penalty equal to 90 days' interest on the amount withdrawn.

## What happens when my fixed-rate account matures?

We will contact you at least 14 days before maturity. If we do not receive instructions, the balance is transferred to an Easy Access Saver on the maturity date.

## Will I be told if my variable interest rate changes?

Yes. We give at least 14 days' notice before reducing a variable rate, and notify you of increases within 3 business days of the change taking effect. You may close or switch your account without penalty during the notice period.

## Is there a limit on how much I can save?

The maximum balance for the Easy Access Saver and Bonus Saver is $250,000 per customer. The 12-Month Fixed Saver accepts deposits of up to $500,000.

## Are my savings protected?

In this fictional scenario, deposits at Fernhollow Bank are protected by a national deposit guarantee scheme up to $250,000 per depositor. Balances above this limit are not covered.

## How do I open a savings account?

Existing Fernhollow customers can open a savings account in the mobile app or online banking in a few minutes. New customers must first complete identity verification as described in the Account Opening and Identity Verification policy.
