# Card Transaction Disputes and Chargebacks

*Fernhollow Bank is a fictional bank. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.*

Document ID: FHB-POL-003 · Effective date: 15 January 2026 · Owner: Card Operations

## Scope

This policy covers disputes about transactions on Fernhollow debit and credit cards, including unauthorised transactions, goods or services not received, duplicate charges and incorrect amounts. Suspected fraud should be reported immediately as described in the Fraud and Account Security policy.

## Before Raising a Dispute

For non-fraud disputes, customers should first try to resolve the issue directly with the merchant, for example by requesting a refund or cancellation. Customers should keep evidence of this contact, such as emails or chat transcripts.

## How to Raise a Dispute

Disputes can be raised:

- In the mobile app, by selecting the transaction and choosing **Dispute this transaction**.
- In online banking under **Cards > Disputes**.
- By calling Card Services on the number printed on the back of the card.

## Time Limits

- Disputes must be raised within **120 days** of the transaction date, or within 120 days of the expected delivery date for goods or services not received.
- Disputes about unauthorised transactions should be reported as soon as the customer becomes aware of them. Customer liability for unauthorised debit card transactions may increase if they are reported more than 60 days after the statement on which they first appeared.

## Information Required

Customers will be asked for:

- The transaction date, amount and merchant name.
- The reason for the dispute.
- Any supporting evidence, such as receipts, cancellation confirmations or correspondence with the merchant.

## Provisional Credit

For disputes on debit cards, Fernhollow will normally issue a provisional credit for the disputed amount within **10 business days** of receiving the dispute, while the investigation continues. If the investigation finds that the transaction was valid, the provisional credit is reversed and the customer is notified at least 5 business days before the reversal.

## Investigation and Outcome

Fernhollow aims to complete investigations within 45 days. Disputes involving international transactions or new accounts may take up to 90 days. The customer is notified of the outcome in writing. If the dispute is upheld, any provisional credit becomes permanent and related fees or interest are refunded.

## Chargebacks

Where appropriate, Fernhollow raises a chargeback with the card network on the customer's behalf. Card network rules determine whether a chargeback succeeds. A merchant may provide evidence to contest a chargeback, in which case Fernhollow may request further information from the customer.
